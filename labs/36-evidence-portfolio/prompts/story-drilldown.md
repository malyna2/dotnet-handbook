# Prompt — story drill-down (before a live run)

Use this on a **written** STAR worksheet before you rehearse it aloud. It finds the holes an interviewer will find. Anonymise first: no customer names, internal system names or unpublished figures.

```text
Below is a STAR story I plan to tell in a Senior .NET Backend Engineer
interview. Act as a sceptical, experienced interviewer reading it in advance.
Do not rewrite it and do not praise it.

1. List the five follow-up questions you would most want to ask, in the order
   you would ask them, each with one line on what weakness it probes.
2. Mark every sentence where I say "we" but the question is about me.
3. Mark every claim with no number, and every number with no baseline, time
   window or source.
4. Tell me which part of the Action is thinnest — where I would run out of
   detail by the second "why?".
5. Tell me whether the Situation can be understood by someone outside my
   company in 20 seconds. If not, name the words that need explaining.
6. Name the question from this list the story fits best, and one it is often
   misused for: a hard bug; a disagreement; a bad decision; mentoring; tech
   debt; pushing back on scope or a deadline; a decision without complete
   information; an incident I owned; influencing without authority.
```
